Our Story

The people behind the evenings you remember

AO Luxe Events is a small, deliberate team. The same people who take your first call are the ones standing in the room on the day, which is exactly how we like it.

Founder

Founder & Lead Designer

AO Luxe Events began with a simple conviction: that a celebration should feel considered, never rushed, and that luxury is really just care made visible. What started as helping friends and family stage their most important days has grown into a planning team trusted with weddings, milestones and corporate occasions across Nigeria. Every event still passes through the same hands and the same standard it did on day one: class, value and elegance, in that order.

How We Work

Three steps, start to finish

01

We listen first

Before a single vendor is approached we want to know the occasion behind the occasion: who is coming, what matters to you, and what the day needs to feel like.

02

We plan in the open

You see the concept, the layout and the full budget before we commit to anything. No hidden vendor mark-ups, no surprises two weeks out.

03

We carry the day

On the day itself our team runs setup, suppliers, timing and teardown. Your only job is to be present at your own celebration.
